# Portfolio Scope

The portfolio is grounded in the supplied aerial-firefighting industry brief.

## Built portfolio systems

- Wildfire Common Operating Picture (COP) Dashboard
- Debrief Intelligence & Lessons-Learned Platform
- Predictive Maintenance & Fleet Readiness Dashboard

## Industry pain points represented

1. No shared common operating picture
2. Detection-to-action gap
3. Fuels and micro-weather intelligence gaps
4. Reactive aircraft maintenance and fleet downtime
5. Manual / hard-to-reoptimize dispatch and resource allocation
6. Contract, cost and compliance overhead
7. Crew fatigue, staffing shortages and slow training pipelines

The site deliberately distinguishes completed portfolio systems from research-only future opportunities.
